Crypto Lab

Check downloads, lock messages and sign things, right in your browser.

Nothing you type or drop here leaves your browser. Keys made here appear on screen, so use them for testing only.

Check a file

Make sure a download is the real thing. Choose the file, then paste the checksum shown on the page you got it from.

Or check some text instead

Lock a message

Protect a message or a file with a password. Only someone with the same password can unlock it, and a forgotten password can't be recovered.

Sign & verify

A signature proves a message came from you and wasn't changed. You sign with your private key; anyone can check with your public key.
